Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Equaly is run by Ben Majani, a sole trader registered in France (entrepreneur individuel, SIRET 932 714 793 00019), 6 rue de Picardie, 35000 Rennes, France. Contact: hello@getequaly.com. He is the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect

Account: a technical identifier created automatically when you open the app, and the email you give when you create your account (optional). We never ask for a password.

Email news: only if you tick the box, we send you tips and news by email. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Your story: the voice recording or text you share when you start, and its written transcript.

Profile and health information: age, sex, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, pain, health conditions you tell us about, food preferences, and answers to our eating questions.

Daily use: check-ins (mood, energy, cravings), completed actions, weigh-ins, weekly reviews and settings.

Coach messages you send (text, or voice turned into text; voice notes are deleted right after).

Community: your display name, posts, replies, encouragements, reports and blocks, and photos you choose to share on the results wall. Other members can see what you post there.

Subscription status from the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.

Why we use it

To build and adjust your personal plan, show your progress, send the reminders you choose, and run your subscription.

Health information is special-category data. We only process it with your explicit consent, which you give when you start, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

With your separate consent, we may use anonymised and aggregated data to improve our programs. Nothing that identifies you.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

Who helps us

Supabase (database and storage; your data is stored in London, United Kingdom).

OpenAI (turns your voice recording into text).

Anthropic (AI that reads your story and helps shape your plan).

RevenueCat, Apple and Google (subscriptions).

Brevo (emails: only if you joined our waitlist or agreed to receive news).

These providers only process data on our instructions. Where data leaves the UK or EU, we rely on approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. Voice recordings are deleted as soon as they have been turned into text. When you delete your account, everything is deleted within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep (for example, invoices).

Your rights

You can access, correct, download or delete your data at any time (Profile → Privacy & data), withdraw consent, object to processing, or ask us questions at hello@getequaly.com.

You can complain to a data protection authority: the ICO in the UK, or the CNIL in France.

Age

Equaly is for adults aged 18 and over only.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
