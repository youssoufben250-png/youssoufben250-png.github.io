How it works

Two minutes to start, then a few minutes a day. Everything happens at home or on a walk, with nobody watching.

    Get your plan

    Your walks, sessions and food changes are set from what you said: the time you have, your knees, your evenings.

  3. 3

    Do one thing a day

    A 30-second check-in, then one small action. Miss a day and the plan picks up again the next morning.

Questions

Is this another diet?

No. There's no calorie counting and no list of banned foods. You get simple portions, gentle movement and tools for cravings and motivation, at a pace you can keep.

I haven't exercised in years. Is it for me?

Yes, that's who it's built for. Sessions start at 5 minutes, they're low impact, and some can be done sitting down. Walking is the base of every plan.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free version has no time limit: your plan, daily check-ins, walks, starter sessions, weight tracking and 3 coach messages a week. Pro unlocks the rest.

Will I be charged after the 7 days?

No. Your 7 days of Pro need no card. When they end, Pro switches off and you carry on with the free version. You only pay if you choose to subscribe: £14.99 a month, cancel any time.

How fast will I lose weight?

At a healthy pace of about 0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 lb) a week, with a first goal of around 5% of your weight. That's slower than a crash diet, and it's why it lasts.

I have a health condition. Can I use it?

Talk to your doctor before you start, especially if you take treatment for diabetes, have heart problems or high blood pressure, are pregnant or had recent surgery. Equaly is a wellbeing coach and does not give medical advice.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored in London. It is never sold and never used for advertising. You can download or delete all of it from the app at any time.
