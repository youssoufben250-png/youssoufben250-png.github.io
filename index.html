Questions you might have

Is this another diet?

No. No calorie counting, no forbidden foods, no crash plans. You get simple portions, gentle movement and tools for your mindset, at a pace that lasts.

I haven't exercised in years. Is it for me?

That's exactly who it's built for. Sessions start at 5 minutes, are low impact, and some can be done seated. Walking is the base of every plan.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free version has no time limit: your plan, daily check-ins, walks, starter sessions, weight tracking and 3 coach messages a week. Pro unlocks everything else.

Will I be charged after the 7 days?

No. Your 7 days of Pro need no card. When they end, Pro switches off and you carry on with the free version. You only pay if you choose to subscribe: £14.99 a month, cancel anytime.

How fast will I lose weight?

At a healthy pace: about 0.5 to 1 kg a week, with a first goal of around 5% of your weight. Slower than a crash diet, and that's why it lasts.

I have a health condition. Can I use it?

Talk to your doctor before you start, especially with diabetes treatment, heart problems, high blood pressure, pregnancy or recent surgery. Equaly is a wellbeing coach, not medical advice.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is stored in London, never sold and never used for advertising. You can download or delete everything from the app at any time.
