Questions you might have

Is Equaly free?

Yes. The free version is free for as long as you like: your personal plan, daily check-ins, daily walks, starter sessions, weight tracking and your coach 3 times a week. Pro adds the mind tools, the unlimited coach, Check my plate, the community, weekly meal plans and every session.

What happens after my 7 days of Pro?

You choose. Keep using the free version at no cost, or subscribe to Pro for £14.99 a month ($14.99 in the US). Nothing is charged automatically: Pro simply switches off and you carry on with the free version. We'll remind you before your 7 days end.

I haven't exercised in years. Is it for me?

That's exactly who it's built for. Sessions start at 5 minutes, are low impact, and some can be done seated. Check with your doctor before starting, especially if you have a health condition.

Is this another diet?

No. There are no forbidden foods and no crash plans. You get simple, realistic guidance and work on your mindset at the same time.
