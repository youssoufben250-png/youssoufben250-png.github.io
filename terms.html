Not medical advice

Equaly does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace your doctor. Talk to a doctor before starting if you have a health condition, take medication, are pregnant, or have a BMI of 40 or more.

Stop any exercise that causes pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or chest discomfort, and seek medical help.

If you think you may have an eating disorder, please contact a professional. Equaly is not designed to treat eating disorders.
